Pricing · PRICING FOR MARGIN, TURNOVER, CONVERSION AND TENDERS

The right price in every decision, to earn more on every sale

Stop leaving margin on the table. Pricing finds the right price for every SKU, store, session and tender, within your margin rules, and shows the reason behind each suggestion.

For small, mid-sized and large companies.

01PRICE-TO-MARGIN

PRICE-TO-MARGIN

Margin by SKU

The model measures how demand for each SKU reacts to price, projects volume and simulates scenarios until it finds the best range, with the optimal price, a confidence interval and alternatives.
  • —Optimal price and recommended range per SKU
  • —Alternatives for each SKU
  • —Minimum margin and competitive band respected
02PRICE-TO-TURNOVER

PRICE-TO-TURNOVER

Turnover without burning margin

Price and markdown ladder by store, region or cluster, following each product's life cycle, to speed up turnover without destroying margin.
  • progressive markdown
  • by store, region or cluster
03PRICE-TO-CONVERSION

PRICE-TO-CONVERSION

The price for every session

Session signals become a real-time contextual price, always within the allowed price space, and the model learns from every conversion.
  • —Response in milliseconds
  • —Only prices within your margin
  • —Learns from every conversion
04Tenders

Tenders

The bid most likely to win

Each tender item gets a suggested price and the win chance at each level, with the quote as the ceiling and, with cost, the most profitable bid highlighted.
  • win chance at each level
  • margin as the limit
05Explanation

Explanation

What drove each price

The weight of each factor in the suggestion, sales below the typical price highlighted, and the actual result compared with the recommended one.
  • —Weight of each factor in the suggestion
  • —Where price slips today
  • —Result compared with the recommendation
06Data and rules

Data and rules

Your data and your rules

Price and volume history, inventory, competition and demand forecast; you set the minimum margin, the commercial policy and the cadence of each decision.
  • minimum margin as a rule
  • configurable cadence
