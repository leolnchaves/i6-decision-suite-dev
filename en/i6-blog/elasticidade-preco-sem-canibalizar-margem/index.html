Price and elasticity

Price elasticity without eating your margin

Elasticity curves per store cluster let you raise price where demand is rigid and protect volume where it is sensitive.

Average price hides the decision

A single elasticity for the whole chain averages away behaviours that cancel each other out. Cluster by observed behaviour and the pockets appear.

How to structure the test

  1. Group stores by behaviour, not by administrative region.
  2. Estimate the curve per cluster and per price band.
  3. Simulate margin impact before touching the price list.

The signal it worked

Category margin rises without meaningful volume loss in the sensitive clusters.
